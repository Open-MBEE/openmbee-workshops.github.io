Before you register

Which dates and locations should I plan for?

OpenMBEE sessions at INCOSE IW are currently planned for January 30 and February 1 in Torrance. The opening reception is February 3 at JPL, followed by the February 4–5 workshop at Caltech in Pasadena.

Do I need to register for the reception separately?

Yes. Use the separate JPL reception form if you plan to attend. The form notes that advance visitor checking is required.

Is the full program available?

The program includes featured keynotes, Open Space, and hackathon activities. Additional technical sessions are still being planned. See the Caltech program or IW session page for details and links to the evolving wiki agenda.

Built around an open community

OpenMBEE grew from work at ESO, JPL/NASA, and Boeing. Its mission is to build a community for open-source systems engineering, alongside INCOSE and the wider systems engineering profession.
