About IW

The INCOSE International Workshop is a four-day gathering where systems engineers work together in sessions. Unlike INCOSE's annual symposium, IW has no paper, panel, or tutorial presentations, so every OpenMBEE session at IW is built around working with the group.

OpenMBEE's sessions at IW 2027 are still being planned. Check back here as the schedule is confirmed.
